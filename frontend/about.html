Meet the Founder

Soahim Rahman Tasin

Founder & CEO

Soahim Rahman Tasin is a self-driven creative professional with a sharp eye for visual systems and a relentless standard for quality. He started SoSpark Design after recognising a gap in the market: clients wanted world-class creative work but were forced to choose between cost and quality. He decided that was a problem worth solving.

"I started SoSpark because I believed small brands deserved the same quality of creative work as the biggest names in the world. That belief hasn't changed — it's gotten stronger."

With expertise spanning brand identity, web design and development, motion, and social content creation, Soahim leads every major project at SoSpark — ensuring the quality bar is never dropped. He is the creative vision and the standard the entire studio works to.

When he's not designing or building, he's studying the best brands in the world — deconstructing what makes them work, so SoSpark's clients can benefit from those same principles, at any budget.

What We Stand For

Principles that drive
every decision.

01

Craft Over Speed

We'd rather take an extra day and deliver something exceptional than rush and deliver something forgettable. Quality isn't negotiable — it's our baseline.

◈
02

Strategy First

Beautiful work that doesn't perform is decoration. Every design decision is grounded in strategy — what your audience needs to see, feel, and do.

◎
03

Radical Honesty

We tell clients what they need to hear, not what they want to hear. If something won't work, we'll say so — and bring a better idea to the table.

Our Journey

How we got here.

2022

The Spark Ignites

Soahim began freelancing in graphic design and branding, quickly building a reputation for work that went beyond the brief. Early clients saw their engagement double and their brand confidence transform.

2023

Expanding the Stack

Demand grew for web design and development alongside brand work. Soahim expanded his service range, delivering end-to-end creative for clients who wanted everything under one roof.

2024

SoSpark Design is Born

With a growing portfolio, a clear creative vision, and a team beginning to form, SoSpark Design launched as a full-service agency. Six services. One standard.

Now

Building What's Next

SoSpark continues to grow — adding talent, expanding capabilities, and working with brands across industries who want creative work that moves the needle.
